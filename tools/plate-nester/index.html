      Tips
      • Real shapes, not boxes: each model's outline is measured and the next one is slid into the first free spot, so an L-shaped piece or a ring leaves room for small ones beside or inside it. It also tries turning each one.
      • Colours: the 3MF keeps one part per model part with its colour and a filament slot (same colour, same slot), so a multi-colour plate prints as it was designed. Use the STL for a single-colour print.
      • Several plates: when everything does not fit, the rest goes on plate 2, 3 and so on. "All plates" gives one 3MF per plate in a ZIP.
      • In your slicer: the set is centred on the bed, so open it and print. If your slicer re-arranges on import, turn auto-arrange off for this file.
      • Tall and short mixed: printing a plate of tall and short parts together takes as long as the tallest, and a tall one can knock a short one over. Large height differences are better split into their own plates.
      • Tight fits are estimates: the outline grid is cautious by up to 2 mm per side. Print a first plate with spacing 4 mm or more, and check your slicer preview.
