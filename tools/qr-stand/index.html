Etsy Shop Tools
QR code
One QR "pixel" is a module. Bigger modules scan from further away but make a bigger plate. Keep the quiet zone at about 4 modules or more.
Plate
Colours are for the preview and the colour 3MF. Two colours scan far more reliably than one.
Branding (optional)
An icon on its own sits in the upper-right corner when the banner is above. A long title shrinks to fit the plate.
Stand base
Clearance is extra slot width so the printed plate slides in. Tune it to your printer. The plate gets a blank tab at the bottom so the slot never covers the code or banner.
Printing tips
  • Two parts, printed flat: the plate and the base are separate files. Print both exactly as exported, with no supports, then slide the plate into the slot; no glue if the clearance is right.
  • Assembled shows how the two fit together and which way the code faces. It is for checking only, not for printing.
  • Two colours: add one filament change at the height shown above. Raised: the dark QR (and branding) is printed in the last layers. Engraved: dark below, light on top. Or download the 3MF, which has the plate and the artwork as two parts for filament slots 1 and 2.
  • Fit: do a small test print of the base first and adjust the slot clearance. Start at 0.3 mm; use more if the plate is tight, less if it wobbles.
  • Stability: a deeper, wider base and a smaller lean angle make the stand harder to tip. Over about 25 degrees of lean a heavy plate can tip forward.
  • Scanning: always test-scan the printed plate. If it is unreliable, enlarge the modules, raise the error correction, or use a more contrasting colour pair.
