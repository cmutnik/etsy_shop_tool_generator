Which setting should I use?
  • Logo or drawing on a plain background: Black & white with Threshold (auto). For a vector you can scale, choose Line art with Trace shapes.
  • Phone photo of a drawing or page (shadows, uneven light): Adaptive, with a local area about a tenth of the picture's width.
  • Photo to line drawing: Line art with Outline edges. Raise smoothing to ignore texture, raise detail for fainter lines, and thicken the line so it prints.
  • Shaded picture as dots: Dither (PNG only).
  • Stamps and 3D printing: keep shapes bold. Thin lines under about 0.6 mm at print size will not print, so thicken lines or remove specks here, then use the button to send it to the stamp generator.
  • Everything runs in your browser; the picture is never uploaded.
