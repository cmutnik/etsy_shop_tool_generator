Printing & use tips
  • Orientation: the model is already oriented stamp-face-down. Print as-is, with no supports. The face is the first layer, so it comes out flat.
  • Settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.12-0.16 mm layers, 3+ walls, 20%+ infill. PLA or PETG both work. Leave ironing off.
  • Bed: a smooth PEI sheet gives the smoothest stamp face; a textured sheet will transfer its texture to the print.
  • Mirroring: the text is mirrored on the stamp face on purpose, so it stamps the right way round.
  • Ink: use standard water- or pigment-based stamp ink pads. Lightly press, don't rock. If the face is bumpy, sand it flat on fine (400+) paper.
  • Thin details: anything narrower than 0.6 mm is drawn in red in the imprint preview - it may not print or may snap off. Tiny text under ~4 mm tall has the same problem.
  • Logos: SVG gives the cleanest result. For PNG/JPG use a high-contrast image; a transparent PNG is read by its transparency, anything else by brightness.
