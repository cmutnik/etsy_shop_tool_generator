Printing tips
  • Orientation: print flat, exactly as exported, no supports. The spike lies flat on the bed and the letters are on top.
  • Material: PETG, ASA or ABS survive sun and rain far better than PLA, which softens in a hot greenhouse and turns brittle after a season. Dark colours get hotter.
  • Two colours: one filament change at the height shown above. With the 3MF, plate colour in slot 1 and letters in slot 2.
  • Strength: the stake is printed flat, so the layers run along it and it is strongest along its length. Use 3+ walls. Push it into damp soil, not hard ground.
  • Selling a set: the ZIP has one STL per marker if you want to print or reprint a single one. A matching set of 6-12 herb markers sells well. Offer custom names by typing the customer's list here.
