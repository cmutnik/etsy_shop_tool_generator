    What is checked, and what is not
    • Holes and bad geometry: open edges, edges shared by three or more faces, inside-out and flipped faces, duplicates, zero-area triangles, tiny floating fragments. Most slicers print a model with these badly or not at all.
    • Size: fits the bed (also turned a quarter turn), and a hint when the units look wrong.
    • Overhangs: faces that point down more than the angle you set, apart from the ones on the bed. These need supports. Red in the Overhangs view.
    • Thin walls: a ray is cast inward from about 3,000 spread-out points on the surface and measures how far it travels to the opposite wall. This is a sample, not every face, so a very thin fin can slip through. Orange dots in the Thin walls view. Only works on a closed model.
    • Not checked: how your slicer will treat bridges, tiny details below the nozzle width, whether the model is strong enough, or whether the print will warp. Always preview the sliced layers.
