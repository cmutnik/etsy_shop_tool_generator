Printing tips
  • Orientation: print as exported, back on the bed, no supports. The magnet pocket opens onto the bed, so its ceiling is a short bridge that prints fine at this size. A smooth PEI sheet leaves a clean back.
  • Two colours: one filament change at the height of the plate top (shown above). With the 3MF the plate is slot 1 and the raised parts are slot 2.
  • Magnets: glue them in with a drop of super glue or epoxy, all facing the same way (check with a spare). Neodymium magnets are strong and brittle; keep them away from small children, pacemakers and bank cards.
  • Ornaments: the loop's hole needs at least 2.5 mm of plastic round it. Use a thinner plate (3 mm) for a lighter ornament, and a ribbon or cord through the hole.
  • Rim: a raised border hides small wobbles at the edge and makes a plain shape look finished. The artwork is kept inside it automatically.
  • Selling it: custom text and your own drawings are fine. Do not use another artist's picture or a licensed character without permission.
