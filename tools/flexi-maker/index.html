    Size and direction
    Turn the model so the direction you want it to bend along is clear in the preview. The cut planes show where it will be split.
    Joints
    Each cut becomes a joint that prints already assembled. Hook and loop gives each segment a closed loop and links the two at right angles, like a chain, so every pair can swing and twist; the segments end up a link-length apart. Ball and socket puts a ball on a short neck on one segment and a cup over it on the next, like an articulated figure; the cup's mouth is narrower than the ball, so it cannot pull out. Clearance is the space each moving part needs: 0.3-0.4 mm suits most 0.4 mm nozzles, bigger if joints fuse. Laying the model on its side prints the joints with vertical gaps, which prints cleanly; standing it up makes each joint a flat overhang.

    Notes
    • Everything stays in your browser. The file is read on this page and never uploaded.
    • Colours are kept. Each part of a 3MF keeps its colour and filament slot in every segment, and a joint takes the colour of the part it grows out of. Download the 3MF to keep them; an STL has no colour and merges everything into one shell.
    • How it works: the model is cut across at the planes shown and a V-shaped notch is removed around each cut so the pieces can tilt. With hook and loop, a closed loop stands on the lower segment, another hangs from the upper one at right angles, and each passes through the other's opening. With ball and socket, a ball on a short neck (lower segment) is trapped in a cup (upper segment). Where a cut crosses several separate pieces (for example two legs) each gets its own joint.
    • Bend: the bend you ask for is limited if the pieces are too short; the page says so. Fewer segments bend further. Parts that are too thin for a joint stay solid.
    • Printing: print on the side the page shows, no supports inside the joints, a layer height of 0.2 mm or less, and remove the model from the bed gently. If the joints fuse, increase the clearance; if they feel loose, reduce it. Break each joint free by bending it once or twice.
    • The model must be a closed solid. Use the STL / 3MF Modifier's Repair first if the page says a part is not watertight.
    • The cutting engine (manifold-3d, about 0.5 MB) is downloaded the first time you press the button.
