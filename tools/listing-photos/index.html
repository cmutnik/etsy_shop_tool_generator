Tips for listing photos
  • First photo: the hero angle on white or a soft colour, with the whole model in frame, is what shows in search results. Etsy crops the thumbnail to 4:3, so keep the model near the middle.
  • Photo set: a listing can hold up to 20 images. Typical set: hero, front, side, close-up, a size-reference photo, and a photo of the real print. Buyers trust listings that show the real thing, so use these renders alongside real photos of your prints, not instead of them.
  • Be honest: these are renders of the 3D file. Real prints have layer lines and the colour of your filament. Pick the finish and colour closest to the real print, and say in the listing that some photos are renders. Check Etsy's current rules on renders and photo size.
  • Size: 2000 px wide is the minimum worth using; 3000 px is better if you want buyers to zoom in.
  • Transparent PNG: choose "Transparent" to cut the model out, then place it in your own mockup or on a lifestyle photo.
