Printing tips
  • Orientation: print flat, exactly as exported, with no supports. The letters are on the top surface.
  • Two colours: add one filament change at the height shown above (the plate's top). With the 3MF, load the plate colour in slot 1 and the letter colour in slot 2; the colours here are only a preview.
  • Strokes: letters stand 0.8 mm (4 layers at 0.2 mm) so they read clearly. Thin script fonts need a letter height of 12 mm or more to print cleanly with a 0.4 mm nozzle.
  • Letters only (no plate): every letter must touch the next, or the keychain falls apart. Script fonts join; block fonts do not. Pick a plate for those.
  • Loop: the hole needs at least 2.5 mm of wall all round. 4.5 mm holes take most split rings.
  • Selling it: names are personal; typing the customer's name here and exporting takes about a minute. Check a font's licence if you sell the prints commercially. The listed fonts are open-source.
