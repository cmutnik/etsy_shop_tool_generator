Shape
Use a simple, bold silhouette: dark shape on a light background, or a PNG with a transparent background. Anything thinner than about 3 mm will not work as a cutter. Image Prep can clean a picture up first.
Size This is the inside of the cutter, so the cookie comes out this size. The wall and flange add to the outside.
The flange is the lip you press on. Make it wider for a bigger cutter. Set it to 0 for a plain wall.
Printing tips
  • Orientation: print it as exported, flange on the bed and the cutting edge pointing up. No supports. To use it, turn it over: press the flange, edge down.
  • Material: PETG or PLA. For dough, use a food-safe filament and wash by hand: layer lines can hold residue, so treat it as a short-contact cutter rather than for storing food. For clay and craft use it is fine as is.
  • Settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16-0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, slow outer walls. A wall of 1.2 mm prints as exactly 3 lines; a thickness that is not a multiple of 0.4 mm gets gap-filled and can come out weaker.
  • Cutting edge: 0.6 mm is a good balance. 0.4 mm is sharper but breaks more easily.
  • Tight details: sharp inner corners and narrow points are hard to cut cleanly. Rounder shapes cut best. The page warns when a shape is very narrow.
  • Selling it: shapes of characters and logos you do not own can be copyrighted or trademarked. Plain shapes and your own drawings are safe.
