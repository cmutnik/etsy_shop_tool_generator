Plate
Smaller cells keep more detail but make a bigger file. 0.4 mm matches a standard nozzle. Thin areas pass the most light (bright); thick areas pass the least (dark). 0.8 to 3.2 mm suits white PLA and most lamps. A curved plate stands up on its own and wraps round a lamp, with the light on the inside of the curve.
Printing tips
  • Orientation: print it standing up, exactly as exported (the bottom edge is on the bed, the smooth face towards you). Lying flat gives visible layer lines across the image.
  • Filament: plain white PLA, matte if you can. Colour and translucent filament changes the glow. Use a fresh, dry spool.
  • Settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.08-0.12 mm layers, 100% infill (the plate has no inside), 3+ walls, slow outer walls, no supports. A brim helps a tall flat plate stay on the bed. Turn off "ironing" and any top-surface fuzzy skin.
  • Test the lamp: use a bright, cool LED (a phone torch works). Warm or dim lights flatten the shading. Hold the plate a few cm in front of the light.
  • Backlit preview: this is a quick simulation (light fades with thickness), not exact. A real print is slightly softer than the preview.
  • Selling it: a photo of someone else's face or a famous picture can carry copyright and privacy obligations. Check you have permission before listing custom lithophanes.
